SYSTEM // ABOUT DEBUNK

The internet is loud.
Evidence is quieter.

Debunk exists to make checking a claim easier. Instead of asking people to blindly trust a headline, a viral post or an AI answer, Debunk puts the evidence closer to the answer.

What is Debunk?

Debunk is an evidence-focused search experience for settling everyday arguments, checking viral claims and finding out whether something you saw online actually holds up.

You ask a question. Debunk searches relevant information, compares the available evidence and presents a clear result together with the sources used.

"Don't believe everything you see. Check it."

Our approach

Debunk isn't here to tell you what to think. It's here to make it easier to investigate what you're being told.

Sources can be wrong. Information can change. Claims can be complicated. That's why the evidence should always be visible.
