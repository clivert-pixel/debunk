The idea

Settle it with sources, not vibes.

Debunk exists for the moment you're arguing with a friend and there's no easy way to check who's right — and a normal Google search just buries you in unreliable results.

Instead of scraping the whole internet, Debunk figures out what kind of question you're asking and leans on the sources that are actually authoritative for that topic — ESPN and official federations for sports, Forbes and Bloomberg for wealth rankings, WHO and peer-reviewed research for health and science, Reuters and AP for general facts.

If the trusted sources agree, you get a clear "Verified" answer. If they don't — or the fact is time-sensitive — Debunk says so honestly instead of picking a side and pretending it's settled.

Built by

One person, on a phone, with free tools. Debunk is a work in progress — if something looks off, that's the build catching up to the ambition.
